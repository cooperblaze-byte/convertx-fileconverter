About ConvertX

ConvertX is a browser-based file toolkit designed for quick format conversion and media adjustments.

Privacy: image edits run in your browser. Audio and video conversions use FFmpeg.wasm, downloaded from a CDN. Your selected media is processed in browser memory and is not intentionally sent to a ConvertX server. Avoid using public/shared computers for private files.

Advertising & monetisation

This build includes clearly labelled ad placements at the top and bottom of the workspace. They are placeholders, not live ads. To earn revenue, publish the site on a domain you control, apply to an ad network such as Google AdSense, and replace the placeholders with the network's approved code after approval. Do not click your own ads or encourage invalid clicks.

Important: Ad networks have eligibility, privacy, consent and age requirements. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian will need to handle any account and payment arrangements where the provider requires an adult account holder. Add a privacy policy and cookie/consent notice appropriate to your audience and region before running ads.

Limitations

  • Browser memory limits the size of files that can be converted.
  • Some codecs and containers may not be available in the bundled FFmpeg build.
  • 4K upscaling does not create genuine missing detail; it only increases pixel dimensions.
  • Image export supports common browser-readable formats. Animated images may become a still frame.
  • Keep this page open until conversion completes.
